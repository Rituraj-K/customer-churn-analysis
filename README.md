# Customer Churn Analysis & Retention Strategy

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn data to identify key factors affecting customer retention and provide actionable business recommendations.

## Objective

To identify drivers of customer churn and improve customer experience through data-driven insights.

## Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel
* SQL (conceptual)
* Data Analysis & Visualization

## Dataset

Customer dataset containing information on:

* Customer demographics
* Services used
* Monthly charges
* Tenure
* Churn status

## Key Analysis Performed

* Churn rate calculation
* Segmentation analysis by contract type, tenure, pricing, and services
* Root cause analysis of customer churn

## Dashboard

An interactive dashboard was created to visualize:

* Churn rate and KPIs
* Customer segmentation
* Service impact on churn

## Key Insights

* Month-to-month contract customers show highest churn
* Customers with low tenure are more likely to churn
* Higher monthly charges correlate with higher churn
* Lack of support services increases churn probability

## Business Recommendations

* Promote long-term contracts to reduce churn
* Improve onboarding experience for new customers
* Bundle support services to enhance customer satisfaction

## Conclusion

This analysis helps identify high-risk customer segments and provides actionable strategies to improve retention and customer experience.
